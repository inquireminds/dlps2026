Five Ideas That Shape Everything We Do

How learning is structured in Grade 7 & 8 Science and Math.

SDGs

Learning with World Goals

Units link directly to the UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals (e.g., clean water, climate action) so students understand the real-world impact of their learning.

At Home: Your child may ask about water or energy consumption at home.
BTC

Building Thinking Classrooms

Research-based math teaching (Dr. Peter Liljedahl). Students collaborate on tasks standing up at whiteboards in small, randomly selected groups.

At Home: "We stood and solved a problem" means deep active thinking took place!
21C

21st Century Skills

Fostering critical thinking, creativity, communication, digital literacy, and self-directed learning skills as outlined in the Ontario curriculum.

At Home: Ask your child: "How did you figure that out?"
TEAM

Active Collaboration

Students explain ideas, listen actively, and build on each other's work. Every student has a role, while remaining assessed on individual growth.

Assessment: Teamwork builds insight; individual achievement is evaluated independently.
UDL

Universal Design for Learning

Lessons are designed so every student can succeed right from the start. Students have options in how they learn and demonstrate understanding (writing, speaking, drawing, building, video).
