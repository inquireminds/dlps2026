Intermediate Curriculum Night

In the Intermediate Division at David Lewis Public School, our teaching team works collaboratively to nurture curious, capable, and confident learners. We believe every student can achieve excellence when provided with high expectations, robust support, and an environment that fosters self-advocacy, initiative, and a growth mindset.

Self-Advocacy

We guide students to ask questions, seek clarification, and express their learning needs independently as they prepare for high school.

Growth Mindset

Mistakes are treated as valuable learning opportunities. We focus on perseverance, reflection, and continuous personal progress.

Responsibility & Initiative

Students are encouraged to take ownership of their organizational habits, complete tasks on time, and actively participate with pride.

School Routines & Expectations

Supporting smooth transitions, work completion, and student success across Grade 7 & 8.

Locker Use Routine

Lockers are accessed specifically during morning entry, before/after recess, and at dismissal time. Keeping outerwear and backpacks in lockers ensures a safe, clutter-free classroom environment focused on instruction.

Prepared to Learn Daily

Students are expected to arrive in class on time with basic learning supplies: writing utensils, lined paper/notebooks, geometry tools, and charged TDSB Chromebooks ready for learning activities.

Finishing Assignments on Time

Timely submission of classwork and inquiry projects develops strong time-management skills. Students are coached on breaking down larger projects and reaching out for guidance prior to due dates.

Learning Skills & School Motto

We actively reinforce Ontario Learning Skills (Responsibility, Organization, Independent Work, Collaboration, Initiative, Self-Regulation) under our school motto: "Participate with Pride".
